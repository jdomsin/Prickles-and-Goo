Voting from the middle → the spectrum

Two ideas side by side. Vote a few airlines by pushing out from a neutral center with intensity (Section A). Your calls land on a continuous prickly→gooey spectrum (Section B). Mock crowd data; nothing here touches the live app.

A · Vote from the middle

How prickly, how gooey?

The slider starts neutral. Push left for prickly, right for gooey — how far you push is how strongly you feel. No binary flick; degree and direction both count.

B · The spectrum

Where the category lands

Every airline placed on one axis by how the crowd splits — no ranked list, no single "winner" pole. The midline is an even 50/50. Your votes from Section A appear as ◆ markers.
