Prickles & Goo

A collective gut-check: what feels prickly, what feels gooey?

Prickly isn't a knock and gooey isn't a compliment — they're textures, not a scorecard. A thing can be prickly and brilliant (precise, rigorous, sharp) or gooey and a mess. We're mapping how things feel, not whether they're good.

I'm John, a UX and design researcher continually exploring new ways to build. I'm drawn to implicit ways of knowing — and to Alan Watts, who spoke of "prickles" and "goo" as a way to relate to almost anything. This site puts that idea to a vote.

It's a working prototype, so it may break in interesting ways. Questions or feedback are welcome.
