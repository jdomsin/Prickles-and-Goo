# Culturally-charged prickly/gooey — idea list

A running brainstorm. **Nothing here is wired into the app yet** — this is the menu to pick from before integrating.

The "Prickle Rick" move: take something with cultural weight everyone recognizes, and bend it onto the axis — either by **punning the name** (Pickle Rick → Prickle Rick) or by surfacing things that are **inherently** prickly or gooey (Sonic is a literal hedgehog; Slimer is literal goo). Keep two guardrails: stay **universally recognizable** (works across generations), and keep prickly **aspirational** (sharp/brilliant, never crude — see the "prick" note at the bottom).

---

## 1. Daily-drop naming (you flagged this directly)

The recurring hook deserves a name. Candidates:

- **The Daily Goo** / **The Daily Prickle** — the two halves of the drop.
- **Goo of the Day** / **Prickle of the Day** — a single hero pick each morning.
- **The Drop** — neutral umbrella; "Today's Drop."
- **Fresh Goo** (like a produce/press drop) · **The Morning Prickle.**
- Streak/badge flavor: a **"Daily Gooer,"** a **"Prickle a day"** streak.

Strongest: **The Daily Goo / The Daily Prickle** as the two named segments of the drop, with "Today's Drop" as the umbrella.

## 4. Inherent icons — a ready-made "Mascots" category

These don't need a pun — they **embody** a texture. This is the most "obviously gettable" content for a cold user, and a fun standalone set ("Which of these is prickliest?").

**Prickly icons:** Sonic the Hedgehog · Wolverine · Edward Scissorhands · Mr. Pricklepants · a cactus · a porcupine · a sea urchin · a pufferfish · Maleficent · Poison Ivy · Knuckles · a Roomba bumping a wall.

**Gooey icons:** Slimer · The Blob · Flubber · Stay-Puft Marshmallow Man · Venom (the symbiote) · Kirby · Baymax · the Pillsbury Doughboy · Gak/slime · Jell-O · Winnie the Pooh · Olaf.

A head-to-head deck of *known* prickly vs gooey mascots teaches the whole concept in one swipe — arguably the best onboarding category there is.

## Guardrail: the "prick" problem

"Prick" alone reads crude/innuendo. Keep the wordplay on **prickle / prickly** (soft, characterful) — it preserves the aspirational, diamond-bright framing of the prickly pole instead of undercutting it. Skip: anything leaning on "prick" as the joke (Fresh Prince → "Fresh Prick", Pictionary → "Pricktionary", etc.).
